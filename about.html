ABOUT REMY

The extraordinary possibilities
of being.

I write across worlds: literary and speculative fiction, poetry, philosophy, children’s stories, and the occasional outbreak of comic disorder.

Underneath the different forms is a continuing curiosity about consciousness, identity, love, memory, and what becomes possible when we make room for imagination.

My journey has included the U.S. Coast Guard, studies in communications and philosophy at Northern Illinois University, and a lifelong interest in phenomenology, action theory, and cosmology. These threads find their way into the stories, questions, images, and music.

R Remy Miller is my literary home. RGuytano™ is the name under which I explore visual art and music. Spiral Empire Enterprises™ brings these creative expressions together.
